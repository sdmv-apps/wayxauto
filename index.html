What it does

Your dash, with Android Auto on it

WayXAuto runs Android Auto on your phone and sends it to your OMOWAY display whenever your motorcycle is connected. Pick your bike once, then ride.

Wi-Fi, no pairing fuss

Turn on your motorcycle, tap Find and choose its display. No account and no Bluetooth key.

Live preview on your phone

Android Auto starts with the app, so you see exactly what your dash shows before you set off.

Fits the display

Laid out for the wide OMOWAY screen, clear of its clock and overlays, with a smooth, steady picture.

Free & independent

No ads and no account. Free forever, supported entirely by optional donations.

Install

Installing the APK

WayXAuto is downloaded from this site rather than the Play Store, so Android shows a few safety prompts the first time. They are normal for any app installed this way.

Download and install

  1. Tap Download for Android. If your browser warns that this type of file can be harmful, tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded WayXAuto file from the notification or your Downloads.
  3. Android says your browser isn't allowed to install apps: tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.

Updating? Install the new version over the old one. Your setup and keys are kept.

Play Protect says the app is unknown or unsafe

Play Protect warns about apps that don't come from the Play Store. Tap More details, then Install anyway. If it offers to scan the app, you can let it.

WayXAuto doesn't read your SMS, contacts or notifications, and doesn't use Accessibility. It asks only for what it needs to reach your motorcycle: nearby Wi-Fi devices (on older Android, location, which Android requires for Wi-Fi scanning) and notifications to show that it's running.

To check your download is the original file, compare its SHA-256 with the one on GitHub Releases.

Getting started

Three steps to the dash

A short one-time setup. The app walks you through it and checks each step before you ride.

Install WayXAuto

Download the APK and allow the install from your browser or files app.

Get the keys & start Android Auto's head unit server

Tap Get keys: WayXAuto downloads two free public files and sets them up. Then turn on Android Auto developer mode and start its head unit server. The app shows each step and ticks it off by itself.

Turn on your motorcycle and pick its display

Open WayXAuto, tap Find and choose your motorcycle. Android Auto appears on the dash.

Questions

Setup, explained

Where do the head unit keys come from?

Android Auto only connects to a head unit that presents a certificate. WayXAuto uses the free sample certificate and key from Open Headunit, an open-source Android Auto head unit project on GitHub. They are published openly for testing, so everyone gets the same two files. They aren't secret, contain nothing about you, your phone or your motorcycle, and don't unlock anything. The certificate is labelled “Google-Android-Reference”, the name of that sample identity.

WayXAuto doesn't include them in the app. When you tap Get keys, it downloads them from GitHub, from the exact version WayXAuto was tested with, checks their fingerprints and keeps them inside the app only.

How do I turn on Android Auto developer mode?
  1. In WayXAuto setup, tap Open Android Auto settings.
  2. Scroll to the very bottom and tap Version about 10 times. When Android Auto asks to allow development settings, tap OK.
  3. Tap ⋮ at the top right, then Start head unit server. No ⋮ menu yet? Do step 2 again.
  4. Come back to WayXAuto. The step turns green by itself.

Developer mode stays on. After a phone restart, or if Android Auto stops the server, only step 3 is needed again.

My motorcycle still shows the OMOWAY app

Your motorcycle shows one phone app at a time, so the OMOWAY app has to let go first.

  1. Force stop the OMOWAY app. WayXAuto shows a button that opens its app info.
  2. On your motorcycle, close Navigation and open it again. Android Auto then appears.
